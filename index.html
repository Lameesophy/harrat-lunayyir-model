The 2009 Intrusion

In spring 2009, a dike intrusion beneath Harrat Lunayyir produced one of the most significant seismic swarms ever recorded in Saudi Arabia.

Event geometry and counts: Pallister et al. (2010). Diagram is explanatory, not a reconstruction.

A crack filled with rising molten basalt — approximately 10 km long with a maximum opening of 2–4 m, pushing up from roughly 20 km depth — produced a swarm of roughly 30,000 earthquakes over several months. The largest event, on 19 May 2009, reached ML 5.4 (Mw 5.7).

More than 30,000 people were evacuated from a 40 km radius, including the town of Al Ays. Surface deformation produced a graben roughly 8–10 km long and up to about 1 m deep.

From geology to mathematics

The oscillators represent evolving structural responses — not individual earthquakes.

Physical system Represented as Coupled-oscillator model
Magmatic intrusion → Master oscillator: θm(t)
Crustal fault or segment → Slave oscillator: θc(t)
Response timing → Oscillator phase: θ ∈ [0, 2π)
Stress / fluid-pressure transfer → Coupling strength: K
Collective alignment → Order parameter: r(t): 0 → 1

Sensitivity & Limitations

The best-fit coupling ratio (K/Kc = 1.86) ranges from 0.30 to 6.00 depending on uncertain input parameters.

Key insight: Constraining K from real monitoring data is the outstanding work. This model demonstrates that if the bursts are a physical oscillation, the coupling strength can be estimated — but real data is required to move from hypothesis to evidence.

Governing Equations

The model is based on two coupled ordinary differential equations.

Master Oscillator (Magma Column)

dθm/dt = ωm + ½M² sin(2θm) − η · (dθm/dt)prev + σ · noise

Bernoulli forcing (proportional to M²) keeps the column wagging against viscous damping η.

Slave Oscillator (Brittle Crust)

dθc/dt = ωc + K · c(t) · sin(θm − θc) + σ · noise

Coupling strength K transmits the magma rhythm to the crust. Pore-pressure envelope c(t) gates the coupling.

Order Parameter

r(t) · eiψ(t) = (1/N) · Σj eiθj(t)

Measures collective synchronisation: r = 0 (incoherent), r = 1 (fully phase-locked).

Critical Coupling

Kc = 2 · σω
